Privacy Policy

Fleent Rides is a student carpooling service. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address. When you create an account we collect your full name, student ID, phone number, university and department. This information is stored in a Google Sheet managed by the Fleent Rides team. It is used only to run the service: to let drivers and passengers see each other’s name and phone number once a ride is booked, and for administration and payment reconciliation. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. To ask for your data to be corrected or deleted, contact dayanwaheed460@gmail.com
